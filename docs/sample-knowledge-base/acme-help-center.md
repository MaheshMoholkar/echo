# Acme Inc Help Center

## Orders and shipping

Orders placed before 2 pm IST ship the same business day; later orders ship the next business day. Delivery within India takes 3 to 5 business days. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days, and customs duties are paid by the recipient.

You can track your order from the link in your shipping confirmation email, or under Account > Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Returns and refunds

You can return any product within 30 days of delivery, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging. Start a return from Account > Orders > Return item; we email you a prepaid return label for orders within India.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

## Accounts and login

To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you. The link is valid for 1 hour. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support.

You can change your email address under Account > Settings > Profile. For security, we send a confirmation link to the new address.

## Plans and pricing

Acme Basic is free and includes up to 3 projects. Acme Pro costs 499 rupees per month (or 4,990 rupees per year) and includes unlimited projects, priority support and data export. You can cancel Pro at any time; it stays active until the end of the billing period.

## Contact us

Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm IST, by chat and at support@acme.example. We do not offer phone support.
